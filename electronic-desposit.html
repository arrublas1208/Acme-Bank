Deposit Instructions

Follow these steps for a successful electronic deposit:

  1. Select the **Destination Account** where you want the funds to be deposited.
  2. Enter the exact **Amount to Deposit**.
  3. Choose the **Source of Deposit** (e.g., Check, Cash, Transfer).
  4. If depositing a check, you will be prompted to upload clear images of the **front and back** of the check. Endorse the back of the check with "For Mobile Deposit Only ACME Bank" and your signature.
  5. Add an optional **Memo** for your records.
  6. Review all details carefully and click **Confirm Deposit**.

Deposits made after 5:00 PM EST on a business day, or on weekends/holidays, will be processed on the next business day. Funds availability may vary.
