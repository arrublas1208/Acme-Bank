Withdrawal Instructions

Please read the following instructions carefully before proceeding:

  1. Select the **Source Account** from which you wish to withdraw funds.
  2. Enter the exact **Amount to Withdraw**. Ensure you have sufficient funds.
  3. Choose your **Withdrawal Method**:
    • **ATM Withdrawal (Request Code):** You will receive a unique code via SMS/email to use at any ACME Bank ATM without a card.
    • **Transfer to External Bank Account:** Provide the necessary details for the external bank account. Transfers may take 1-3 business days.
  4. Add an optional **Memo** for your records.
  5. Review all details carefully before clicking **Confirm Withdrawal**.

Daily withdrawal limits may apply. For larger withdrawals or specific needs, please contact customer support.
